About

I like software that people in my own town use. ParkMK helps drivers pay for parking in Bitola. SmartCityZen helps wheelchair users and parents with strollers find a path without obstacles.

I completed two internships at KS Group – Evrotip, where I built REST APIs with Laravel, Angular interfaces and MySQL reports. Alongside development I lead student initiatives at my faculty and sit in the University Senate, so I am used to working with teams, deadlines and institutions.

Databases and SQL are my favorite part of development. I design MySQL schemas, write queries with aggregations and joins, and build reports that turn raw data into something people can read and act on. I write Laravel migrations to keep the database structure under version control, and I work on the relationships between tables and datasets so that data from different sources stays connected and consistent.

I am looking for a junior full-stack or back-end role where I can keep growing in Laravel, PHP and mobile development.

SmartCityZen

Accessibility map for Bitola, UKLO – FICT, since September 2026

An Android app for people with mobility impairments. It plans pedestrian routes that avoid reported obstacles such as potholes, missing ramps and blocked paths. Residents report problems with photos, and the community verifies them.

  • Android
  • Firebase Firestore
  • OpenStreetMap
  • GPS routing
